# Proportional hatch progress

Release 20260924-05, source 7f8368e2860f195eff87b769686c0287314860ec. Active jigsaw-web@3104; rollback 20260924-04. No migrations.

On completed rooms, each account earns (tier base + applicable daily-first 5 + new-picture 5) multiplied by its successful connection count / all successful connection counts. Positive safe-integer counts include departed accounts and guests in the denominator. Guests receive no account reward and their share is not redistributed. The old 3/5/10 action eligibility threshold is replaced by positive contribution. Zero contributors and unfinished rooms earn nothing. Server-recorded snapped drops remain the sole contribution source. Round to six decimal places when awarding, adding and spending progress; feedback deltas are similarly stabilized. Existing balances and settled rooms are not recalculated. First-completion fragments, hatch costs, cosmetic odds and daily quotas are unchanged.
